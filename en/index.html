Good Night, Toys

An interactive bedtime picture book: your child puts the toys to bed, switches off the light and hangs the moon in the window. Then it gets dark, the lullaby grows quieter – and the book closes.

How bedtime goes

Calm, with no time pressure, no points and no failing. Just tap and drag – made for children who can't read yet.

Toys to bed

Every toy has its own sleeping place – with pillow, blanket and everything that belongs.

Light off

When everyone is lying down, your child switches off the light. The room goes quiet.

Moon in the window

Finally the moon goes in the window, the lullaby fades and the book closes.

Frequently asked

Does my child need to read?

No. Everything works with pictures, gestures and sounds.

What does it cost?

The download and the Kids' Room and Farm books are free. All other books come with one single “All Books” purchase – no subscription.

Does the app collect data?

No. No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no network connections. See the app privacy policy.
