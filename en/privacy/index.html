App Privacy Policy

“Good Night, Toys” (iPhone & iPad) – last updated October 2026

In short: The app does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no account, no network connections.

1. Controller

Minh Nguyen Studios. Full company details and address: minh.systems/impressum. Privacy contact:

2. No data collection

  • The app collects no personal data – neither from children nor from parents.
  • There are no analytics or statistics services, no advertising, no tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • The app makes no network requests and works completely offline.
  • There is no user account and no way to share content or communicate with others.

3. Storage on the device

Settings (e.g. volume, language, help level) and progress are stored only locally on your device and are never sent to us. Deleting the app removes this data.

4. In-app purchase

The “All Books” purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). We never receive payment details, your Apple ID or any other personal data. Apple's privacy policy applies. The purchase area is behind a parental gate.

5. Children

The app is designed for children aged 3 to 6 and meets the requirements of the App Store Kids category. Because we collect no data at all, no children's data is processed within the meaning of COPPA (USA) or Art. 8 GDPR (GDPR-K). External links, purchases and settings are only reachable through the parental gate.

6. Your rights

As we do not process any personal data about you or your child, there is nothing we could disclose, correct or delete. You can contact us with any questions at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

7. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security purposes; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The website uses no cookies, no analytics and loads no external fonts or content from third-party servers. More: Minh Nguyen Studios privacy policy.

8. Changes

If the app ever changes in a way that affects privacy, we will update this policy beforehand.
