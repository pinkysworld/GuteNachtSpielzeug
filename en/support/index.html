Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the parent area (see below) and tap “Restore purchase”. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID that bought “All Books”.

How do I open the parent area?

Press and hold the gear in the top-left corner for 1.5 seconds. A number appears written as a word (e.g. “seven”) – tap the matching digit. In the parent area you can set toys per round, help level, music and sound volume, “only one round this session” and the language, restore purchases and see the credits.

Does Family Sharing work?

Yes. “All Books” supports Family Sharing. Family members tap “Restore purchase” in the parent area.

Which languages are available?

German, English, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese, Swedish, Danish, Norwegian, Japanese, Korean and Chinese. More languages are coming. You can switch the language in the parent area.

What data does the app collect?

None. No account, no analytics, no ads, no network connections. Settings stay on the device only. Details in the app privacy policy.

How do I reset the app or delete all data?

Simply delete the app from your device – that removes all settings and progress. You can restore your purchase for free at any time after reinstalling.

What does it cost?

The download, the Kids' Room and the Farm are free. One single “All Books” purchase (€3.99 in Germany, adjusted by country) unlocks every room forever – including future ones. No subscription, no ads.
